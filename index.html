How to run the test

  • One ad set per service, with all of its Test now ads (video, static and carousel together). Broad audience and Advantage+ placements. Let Meta split the budget, and don't force it evenly.
  • Hands off for 7 days. Don't edit a live ad; duplicate it instead. Editing restarts learning.
  • Judge on booked intro calls per ad. Ad Lab matches each booking to its ad through the id-based URL parameters. Meta can't optimize health ads on bookings, so your bookings are the real score, not Meta's "results".
  • Menopause leads with price. Meta's menopause bookings have mostly been low-intent, so the price card (M3) goes first, and every menopause ad's text now states the program price ($895 + HST for the first 3 months). Fewer but better bookings is the goal.
  • Day 7: pause any ad that spent $40 or more with no bookings and a CTR under half the ad set's average. If Meta barely spent on an ad (under $10), it isn't a loser yet: give it a second week or swap in a Next up ad.
  • Day 14: replace the bottom two in each service with Next up ads. Make two new variations of the winning angle (new image, same message).
  • Fatigue: when an ad's frequency passes about 3, or its CTR drops about 30% from week one, swap in its Twin.
  • Turn off the old ads when these go live: the June menopause statics (their link 404s), the member-testimonial ad and the "73%" ad.

Budget guide

Per service, per dayLaunch
$50–60+All six Test now ads. Roughly $8–10 a day each gives every ad a fair week.

Hormozi pass · Oct 13, refined Oct 14

Why these words

Every ad was reviewed the way Alex Hormozi reviews ads: would a stranger stop, know it's for them, want the outcome, believe it, and know the next step? These are the lines that changed on the image. All primary text and headlines were rewritten to the same rules.

Hook, then outcomeThe image line has to stop the scroll and promise something concrete. "Sick today, seen tomorrow" beats "a new kind of clinic".
Cut wait and effortValue goes up when time and effort go down: "start within days", "no referral", "free 15-minute call".
Benefits over featuresNot "body composition scans": "see what's working". Not "a written plan": "know exactly what to do".
Make the price small$1,495 a year is about $4 a day. Every longevity headline or description carries one of the two.
One offer everywhereImage, primary text, headline and button all point at the same thing: a free 15-minute call, no commitment.
Qualify on priceEvery menopause ad's text names the program price ($895 + HST to start), so the free call attracts people who are ready for private care.
Readable on a phoneNothing smaller than about 10px on a phone. Unreadable labels were removed, and contrast behind every sentence is measured at 4.5:1 or better.
Only true scarcity"We cap membership" and "a small panel of patients" both come from the clinic's own pages. Nothing is invented.

Launch in six steps

Repeat for each ad. Every card below has its own files and copy buttons.

  1. Download the ad's two files: the 4:5 Feed file and the 9:16 Stories + Reels file. Carousels have five 4:5 cards.
  2. Open the campaign and go to the right ad set: Menopause or Longevity membership. Start with the ads marked Test now (see What to test). Every ad for a service goes in that service's ad set, video and static alike.
  3. Create ad. Name it with the ID on its card (e.g. LV2-T, M3, MB1). Identity: the Optimal Facebook Page and @beoptimal.ca.
  4. Media: upload the 4:5 file, then customize placements, choose Stories and Reels, and swap in the 9:16 file.
  5. Text: paste the primary text, headline and description with the Copy buttons. CTA Book now. Paste the website URL and URL parameters from the settings box.
  6. Videos only: upload the thumbnail jpg, and turn off auto captions (ours are burned in), Advantage+ music and creative enhancements. Then publish, and judge after 7 days using the rules in What to test.
