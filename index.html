    How to launch

    Reference for whoever uploads. Open what you need.

    Go-live checklist
    Upload an ad in Meta Ads Manager
    1. Download the ad's three files: 1:1 (Feed), 9:16 (Stories + Reels) and 1.91:1 (right column, in-stream, search).
    2. Go to the right ad set: Menopause, Longevity, Cycle & Hormone Care Set 1 or Set 2, or the IV iron campaign. Start with the ads marked Test now or Run now.
    3. Create ad and name it with the ID on its card (e.g. MV3-T, M3). Identity: the Optimal Facebook Page and @beoptimal.ca.
    4. Media: upload the 1:1 file, then customize placements: Stories and Reels get the 9:16 file; right column, search and in-stream get the 1.91:1 file.
    5. Text: paste the primary text, headline and description with the Copy buttons. Use the service's website URL, URL parameters and CTA from the box at the top of its section.
    6. Videos: upload the matching thumbnail, and turn off auto captions (ours are burned in), Advantage+ music and creative enhancements. Leave Meta's AI label off. Publish, then leave it alone for 7 days.

    Why these words (the Hormozi pass, Oct 13)

    Each image line was reviewed the way Alex Hormozi reviews ads: would a stranger stop, know it's for them, want the outcome, believe it, and know the next step? These lines changed.

    LB1 Test now · #4

    Barrie has a new kind of health clinic.

    Your own Nurse Practitioner in Barrie, with same or next-day visits.

    Vague becomes a specific outcome. “New kind of clinic” says nothing; your own NP and next-day visits is the dream outcome.

    LB2 Test now · #5

    Same or next-day visits when something can’t wait.

    Sick today, seen tomorrow, by an NP who knows you.

    A scene the reader has lived, plus the relationship. Time delay goes to almost zero.

    LB3 Test now · #6

    Your body composition, measured every 90 days.

    Your muscle, measured every 90 days.

    Concrete beats abstract: it names the one thing worth measuring, and the copy explains why muscle matters more each decade.

    LB4 Next up

    Every member gets a written 12-month plan.

    Know exactly what to do for your health this year.

    Sell the certainty, not the document.

    LB5 Twin of L1

    Your own Nurse Practitioner for $1,495 a year.

    Your own Nurse Practitioner for about $4 a day.

    The same price reframed per day ($1,495 ÷ 365).

    LB6 Twin of L6

    Medically necessary labs are still covered by OHIP.

    OHIP still covers your medically necessary labs.

    Lead with the reassurance: the objection is answered in the first three words.

    MB2 Test now · #4

    Brain fog is a common perimenopause symptom.

    Brain fog can be perimenopause, not just a busy life.

    Names the wrong explanation she’s been giving herself, then reframes it. Curiosity, not a textbook line.

    MB3 Next up

    Menopause care in Barrie, led by a Nurse Practitioner.

    Start menopause care in Barrie within days.

    Description becomes outcome plus speed. Cutting the time delay raises perceived value.

    MB4 Next up

    Hot flashes don’t wait for a good time.

    Hot flashes don’t wait, and neither should you.

    Keeps the relatable hook and adds the push to act.

    MB5 Test now · #3

    No referral or family doctor needed.

    Menopause care without a referral or a family doctor.

    Says what it is in the first word. The old line never mentioned menopause, so a stranger couldn't tell what was on offer.

    Normal is a range. We talk about where you sit in it.

    Normal is a range, and we look at where you sit in it.

    Same idea, held to one sentence.
